Establishment & Growth

Our Legacy In The
Making

The New York Tamil Development Council (NYTDC) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit standing as a beacon for the Tamil diaspora.

Mission

To unite the Tamil community of Staten Island by preserving and promoting Tamil heritage, language, art, and culture through educational, cultural, and charitable initiatives.

Unified Community

Vision

To build a vibrant and inclusive Tamil community center that serves as a home for cultural expression, lifelong learning, and social connection for future generations.

Future Legacy

Purpose

To establish a cultural hub for arts and education, promote Tamil language fluency, and provide social services that strengthen the diaspora's well-being.

Holistic Growth

The Symbolism of Our Identity

1. Red and Green Colors

Represent the colors of the Karthikai Poo, symbolizing resilience and cultural pride.

2. Handshake (ஒற்றுமை)

Signifies unity and cooperation, representing our commitment to bringing together Tamils under one shared vision.

3. Palm Tree

A timeless emblem of Tamil identity, symbolizing endurance, self-sufficiency, and multifaceted culture.

4. Agriculture & Roots

Farmer, Cows, and Crops honor the farmers whose labor sustains life, humility, and perseverance.

5. Rising Sun

Embodies hope and the continuous rise of Tamil culture in new horizons.

6. Our Motto

அறத்திற்கே அன்புசார் (Araththirkke Anbusaar); true compassion and unity are rooted in virtue.
