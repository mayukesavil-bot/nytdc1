Who We Are

More Than Just A Community Center

The New York Tamil Development Council (NYTDC) began with a simple yet powerful idea: to create a dedicated space where Tamil families in New York could connect, celebrate, and grow.

Founded in 2025, we are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization committed to the preservation of our ancient language and culture while fostering a modern, progressive community for our youth. Our motto, "அறத்திற்கே அன்புசார்" (True love is based on virtue), guides every initiative we undertake.

Lukshmi Hernandez

Board Member

From the time before I was able to walk, my parents taught me the value of my Tamil heritage. Resilient and unwavering, our people have found home, refuge, and community in Staten Island.

From the time before I was able to walk, my parents taught me the value of my Tamil heritage. Resilient and unwavering, our people have found home, refuge, and community in Staten Island. Afterall, we have the largest population of the Ilankai Tamil diaspora in the nation. Having a gathering ground to share language and art, give to our children, learn from our elders, and create meaningful change for our community is what drew me to the mission of NYTDC.
I grew up in Tamil school, sangeetham classes, and bharatanatyam lessons; my parents fiercely protecting what they knew could so easily be lost in one generation. Our friends were familiar, sharing experiences, raised by parents torn between their original and new home. Our community became our family when our parents’ families were separated by oceans. My dance teacher became my loving aunt, my carnatic violin teacher another, and my brothers' Tamil school friends became my little brothers as well. The beauty of this diaspora was built from pain but created such unique vibrancy and sense of belonging exemplified in our community.
Although this was my experience growing up, after college, the landscape was different. Separations in community, childhood friends growing apart, and personal loss of language fluency made finding home in our Tamil spaces difficult and clumsy. Where had the space where I grown in gone? My greatest connection to my Tamil heritage- my beloved Pati- passed in the Spring of 2020. Without her, I lost hearing our language, eating our food, and listening to vivid stories of our people. I was lost.
I ventured to regrow community by combining my two greatest loves- bharatanatyam and teaching. By this time I was newly entrenched and totally absorbed in motherhood. I started my dance school three months after the birth of my first child. I so tremendously craved my passions that felt abandoned in the postpartum period. Prior to motherhood, I was a chemistry teacher and lecturer and also an avid bharatanatyam practitioner and student. The opportunity to build this new space of heritage and home was the gift I was searching for. Creating room for our class of young Tamil girls to show joyous praise and appreciation for their culture through dance has been transformative. How can I now extend this energy outward, bringing spaces of Tamil joy to our entire community? This newest endeavor is what brings me here: the New York Tamil Development Council. We have untapped potential. We have brilliant students, dedicated healthcare workers, caring educators, mothers and fathers, wise elderly; we have an innumerable amount of talented and skilled individuals. Imagine what it would look like for all of it to come together for the shared goal of celebrating and promoting our rich heritage? That is my goal and I am hopeful it will come to life with the NYTDC.

-Lukshmi Hernandez-

Our Committees

1. EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

The Executive Committee (EC) shall manage the organization's affairs.

  • Be a member of New York Tamil Development Council
  • To advance the purpose and objectives of the Corporation
  • Act with diligence, honesty, good faith, and in the best interests of the Corporation
  • Regularly attend meetings of the Board and the meetings of any Board committees to which they have been appointed
  • Engage the Tamil community and other stakeholders in Staten Island
  • Liaison with stakeholders that align with the Purpose and Objectives of the TDC and values found under the Basic Qualifications for Membership

2. ADVISORY COMMITTEE

  • Provide expert advice and guidance to the board and executive team
  • Support strategic planning and organizational growth
  • Help with networking and building community relationships
  • Conduct and oversee the NYTDC Election process

3. FINANCE & FUNDRAISING

  • Helps develop and monitor the annual budget
  • Ensures compliance with financial policies and transparent reporting
  • Plan and executes fundraising events and campaigns
  • Pursues sponsorships, partnerships, and grant opportunities

4. BUSINESS & MARKETING

  • Promote the organization's events and initiatives
  • Manage social media accounts and digital content
  • Oversee branding and public messaging
  • Handle external outreach
  • Support other committees with marketing needs
  • Create and develop social networking events
  • Foster connections for potential partnerships with external organizations and businesses

5. CULTURAL & ARTS

  • Promotes and preserves cultural heritage and artistic expression
  • Organizes cultural programs, performances, and exhibitions
  • Hosts workshops and events showcasing community arts
  • Collaborates with local artists, performers, cultural groups, and nonprofit organizations
  • Encourages community involvement through arts and culture

6. COMMUNITY SERVICE

  • Identifies and addresses community needs
  • Plans and coordinates volunteer activities and service projects
  • Organizes initiatives such as food drives, health fairs, and outreach events
  • Supports vulnerable and underserved community members
  • Promotes equity and community well-being

7. SPORTS COMMITTEE

  • Organizes sports leagues, tournaments, and fitness events
  • Coordinates recreational activities for all age groups
  • Partners with coaches, trainers, and local facilities
  • Promotes inclusivity and community engagement through sports

8. YOUTH & EDUCATION

  • Supports academic and personal development of youth
  • Organizes tutoring, mentorship, and leadership programs
  • Develops a Tamil Language education program for all ages
  • Plans youth-focused events and workshops
  • Collaborates with schools, parents, and community groups
  • Encourages educational success, cultural pride, and future achievement
