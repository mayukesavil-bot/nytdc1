Who We Are

More Than Just A Community Center

The New York Tamil Development Council (NYTDC) began with a simple yet powerful idea: to create a dedicated space where Tamil families in Staten Island could connect, celebrate, and grow.

Founded in 2025, we are a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization committed to the preservation of our ancient language and culture while fostering a modern, progressive community for our youth. Our motto, "அறத்திற்கே அன்புசார்" (True love is based on virtue), guides every initiative we undertake.

Brabthan Jeevakumar, P.E.

Secretary

New York State-licensed Professional Engineer and Project CEO at MTA...

Brabthan Jeevakumar (Prabu) was born in Jaffna, Sri Lanka, raised in Colombo, and has called Staten Island home since 2005. A dedicated husband and father of three, he holds a BSc degree in Engineering Science from the CUNY College of Staten Island. He is a New York State–licensed Professional Engineer, a Certified Construction Manager (CCM), and a Project Management Professional (PMP).

Brabthan serves as a Project Chief Executive Officer / Assistant Director at MTA Construction & Development, where he leads the delivery of major infrastructure projects across New York City. With recognized strengths in engineering, construction management, project leadership, and team development, he brings a strong foundation of integrity, professionalism, and public service to his role as Secretary of NYTDC. Outside of work, Brabthan enjoys sports—especially cricket and volleyball—and is a member of the Staten Island Tamil Volleyball Club. He also loves movies, music, traveling, and spending time with friends.

Deeply rooted in Tamil values and culture, Brabthan is inspired by the words of poet Subramania Bharathiyar: “எண்ணியமுடிதல் வேண்டும், நல்லவே எண்ணல் வேண்டும்.” Guided by this philosophy, he is honored to serve on the NYTDC Executive Committee and to contribute to the creation of New York’s first-ever Tamil Community Center. As a proud founding member of NYTDC, Brabthan is committed to preserving Tamil heritage, strengthening community connections, and building a lasting home where future generations can learn, grow, and thrive.

Josica Ganesamurthy

Board Member

Seasoned software engineer and tech advocate born on Staten Island...

Josica Ganesamurthy is a seasoned software engineer and technologist with a career grounded in engineering and technical client partnership, primarily within the advertising technology sector. She has held roles at Microsoft, AppNexus, Xandr, and Foursquare, contributing to large-scale systems while taking full ownership of designing, building, and launching client-facing applications and internal tools.

Born and raised on Staten Island, Josica grew up deeply engaged in Tamil cultural arts and education. She studied Tamil language through the Staten Island Tamil School at Snug Harbor and trained in Bharatanatyam, experiences that shaped her strong connection to culture, community, and service from an early age.

Before entering the tech industry, Josica served as a Technology Instructor with the New York Public Library’s TechConnect program, where she taught front-end development, digital literacy, and youth-focused game development. In addition to project- and series-based programs, she led regularly scheduled classes from beginner to advanced levels across multiple library branches, helping equip New Yorkers with practical, accessible technology skills.

Throughout her career, Josica has been an advocate for increasing representation in technology. She has worked with Girls Who Code across multiple organizations to mentor students, develop workshops, and teach coding skills, while also supporting broader initiatives focused on women, girls, and underrepresented communities. She previously served as the Xandr chapter co-lead for Microsoft’s Asians employee resource group and as co-founder and president of her college’s South Asian student association.

Josica brings her technical expertise, cultural grounding, and community-focused leadership to support the mission and strategic goals of the organization.

Janany Jeyakumar, PharmD

Board Member

Pharmacist and community leader dedicated to cultural preservation...

Born and raised in Staten Island, Janany grew up deeply connected to the local Tamil community, where cultural traditions, language, and collective experiences played an integral role in shaping her early identity. From a young age, she was actively involved in Staten Island Tamil Arivaalayam, later returning as social media coordinator to support the next generation of learners. She also learned Carnatic music and participated in netball through various youth organizations, fostering a lifelong appreciation for Tamil arts, heritage, and community engagement. These formative experiences instilled in her both a strong sense of cultural pride and a deep commitment to service and leadership.

Janany earned her Doctor of Pharmacy degree, with a minor in Public Health, as well as her Bachelor’s in Pharmaceutical Sciences from Albany College of Pharmacy and Health Sciences. During pharmacy school, she cultivated her leadership skills through involvement in a variety of professional organizations and committees. As a pharmacist, she is dedicated to advancing patient care, promoting health literacy, and contributing to health education within clinical practice. Her professional experiences have reinforced the importance of community involvement, mentorship, and supporting the cultural foundations that shaped her upbringing.

Janany brings a multidisciplinary skillset to the Executive Committee, drawing on her extensive experiences in community service, cultural education, and clinical practice. Her leadership roles in youth mentorship and outreach initiatives demonstrate her ability to engage community members and support organizational growth. As a pharmacist and current PGY-1 resident, she offers strengths in communication, health education, project management, and systematic problem-solving. These competencies enable her to support the Committee’s strategic goals through culturally informed perspectives, evidence-based decision-making, and a long-standing commitment to advancing the Tamil community in Staten Island.

Through her work with the New York Tamil Development Council, Janany aims to contribute to cultural preservation, expand youth engagement, and strengthen opportunities for community collaboration. She is committed to helping build a vibrant, unified, and forward-looking future for Tamils in Staten Island.

Gowsika Jeevananthan

Board Member

Lead Medical Technologist and cancer survivor passionate about Tamil culture...

Gowsika Jeevananthan (Gowsi) was born in Nainativu, Sri Lanka and moved to Staten Island, New York in 2006, where she embarked on a journey of education and professional growth. She holds a bachelor’s degree in Medical Laboratory Technology from the College of Staten Island and a master’s degree in Laboratory Management from Hunter College. She is licensed as a Medical Laboratory Scientist through the American Society of Clinical Pathologists (ASCP MLS). She is currently working with the U.S. Veterans Affairs and serves as a Lead Medical Technologist at NYC Health + Hospitals.

Gowsi is a twelve-year eye cancer survivor, as well as a loving wife and mother of two.

Gowsi is deeply committed to advancing community, culture, education, and social justice initiatives. She is proud to be an active member of the New York Tamil Development Council, where she is dedicated to promoting Tamil culture, language, and sports. Her passion is to preserve and grow our rich heritage while supporting community development, guided by the motto “உடல் மண்ணுக்கு, உயிர் தமிழுக்கு” “Body to the soil, soul/life to Tamil.” She brings her leadership full circle to empower future generations.

Gowsi has also volunteered with the American Cancer Society and with NOW WOW Charity.
